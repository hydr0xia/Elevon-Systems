Foreign Eyes, Foreign Brains[cite: 1]

1. Machines Can't See[cite: 1]

Drones & robots hit ultra-thin wires and obstacles cameras and LiDAR simply miss.[cite: 1]

Optical sensors fail in fog, dust and glare; LiDAR is heavy and power-hungry. A single wire strike destroys the aircraft - the #1 blocker to safe BVLOS.[cite: 1]

2. The Chips Are All Imported[cite: 1]
